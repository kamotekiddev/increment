# 01: Design the splash screen

**What to build:** A splash screen design on the design canvas, for the owner to approve. Starting point: the splash already in the owner's working tree (white Steps bars on accent `#5B3DF5`, see `docs/design-system.md`, Logo and app icon). The design decides whether the plain mark is enough or the splash needs more (for example a wordmark), and how it hands over to the first screen (Welcome on a fresh install, Today after). Light and dark must both look right.

**Status:** ready-for-human
