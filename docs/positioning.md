## Our differences
1. **Progressive overload is free.** The core promise isn't behind a paywall.
2. **Transparent rules you control**: pick the rule per exercise and see the result before saving. No black-box AI.
3. **No subscription at all**: one price, clearly below competitors' lifetime prices.
4. **Your data is yours**: no account, everything on the phone, backups as files you hold.

## Where competitors are ahead
- Alpha Progression has about 795 exercises with videos; we launch with written how-tos.
- Alpha Progression also rounds to your gym's weights.
- Fitbod generates whole workouts; many apps offer cloud sync across devices. We do neither.
