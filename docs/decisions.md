# Decisions

## Decided
- **Name**: Increment (formerly "Overload"; that name is taken in the stores).
- **Platform**: iOS and Android, built with Expo (React Native).
- **Offline, no account, no backend.**
- **Monetization**: a one-time Pro unlock with no subscription.
- **Pro price** (owner decision 2026-11-24, "for now"): ₱300, set as the Philippine peso base price in App Store Connect and Play Console. Each store fills in other countries' prices from it, and the app shows whatever localized price the store returns, so changing it later needs no app update. If ₱300 isn't one of Apple's peso price points, use the nearest one.
- **Backups are Pro-only**; restore is offered during onboarding for returning customers.
- **Units**: a global kg/lb preference, display only. A set is stored as typed, with its unit; Targets are kg, and a routine exercise's Jump is saved per unit in that unit. See [ADR 0003](adr/0003-sets-store-the-weight-as-typed.md).
- **Exercise library source**: a curated subset (178 exercises at the start of Phase 0) of the public-domain [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (Unlicense), text instructions only, with a load type on each exercise.
- **Weights**: a set stores the number typed and its unit (owner decision 2026-11-21, [ADR 0003](adr/0003-sets-store-the-weight-as-typed.md), superseding [ADR 0001](adr/0001-weights-stored-in-kg.md) for sets). Only weights about to be lifted (pre-fills, the Last column, Targets) are rounded to the Jump, and only when they came from the other unit; history shows a set in the other unit as "≈20.4 kg", one decimal. The rounding step is the routine exercise's Jump (see "Jump per routine exercise" below). Backup is still Phase 3; its file carries each set's unit. See `.scratch/phase-2/set-units/spec.md`.
- **Progression**: earned after 3 clean workouts in a row (fixed; the lifter cannot change it, owner decision 2026-11-21); the lifter chooses whether to accept the step. The step, miss handling and deload are free for everyone (owner decision 2026-11-21).
- **Free routine limit**: 2. Adding a third shows the limit pop-up.
- **Routines**: deleting a routine deletes its workouts, and Target history is per routine; see [ADR 0002](adr/0002-deleting-a-routine-deletes-its-workouts.md).
- **Visual direction**: minimal light UI, neutral base, purple accent `#5B3DF5`, Sora font.
- **UI components**: Text and Button are plain React Native on our tokens, because `@expo/ui` Text ignores custom fonts on iOS and its Button draws a system blue box (checked in the Phase 0 spike). `@expo/ui` is used for the bottom sheet, switch and picker.
- **Jump per routine exercise** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-21, replacing the Jump per equipment of 2026-11-19 and Phase 1's fixed 2.5 kg): each routine exercise has its own Jump, one in kg (`step_kg`) and one in lb (`step_lb`), each saved in its own unit and never converted into the other. Both are empty until the lifter sets them, and an empty step is 2.5 kg or 5 lb for every exercise (as StrongLifts does), so a dumbbell curl and a leg press step by 2.5 kg / 5 lb until changed. The engine steps by the one for the current unit; switching unit never writes either, so a 2 kg curl in kg is 5 lb in lb (its own default) and 2 kg again back in kg. A workout whose routine exercise was deleted uses the default. The Weight jumps screen, its Settings row and the per-equipment Jumps are gone; the saved Jumps were dropped without copying (migration 0010; the app was not released). Why: one Jump per equipment broke every exercise on that equipment when one rack differed, and moving a Jump in lb changed it in kg (5 lb saved as 2.27 kg showed as 2.5 kg). Exercises done with or without added weight stay split into a bodyweight exercise and a weighted twin. See `.scratch/phase-2/exercise-steps/spec.md`.
- **No unit switch on the live workout screen** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-20): the `kg | lb` pill beside "Exercise N of M" is removed. Switching mid-workout left checked sets that could not be loaded in the other unit (10 lb shown as 4.54 kg) and open sets that needed their own snap, for a case that is rare: the lifter picks a unit once, in onboarding, and can change it in Settings. Checked sets are never rewritten, so they keep their number and unit. A Settings change while a workout is open (updated 2026-11-21, ADR 0003): a row the lifter has neither typed nor checked shows in the new unit, rounded to the Jump (100 kg is 220 lb), and is saved that way when checked; typed and checked rows keep their saved number and unit. The switch itself writes nothing. See `.scratch/phase-2/settings-and-units/spec.md`.
- **A Target set in the other unit rounds down to the Jump's grid** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-19; rounding down replaced nearest on 2026-11-21): after a unit switch, a Target that is not a multiple of the current unit's Jump could not be loaded, so it was never hit and progression stuck (a 10 kg dumbbell Target shows as 22.05 lb on a 20 / 25 lb rack). Only a Target set in another unit than the current one snaps, to the largest multiple of the Jump at or below it, with a small tolerance so a Target already on the grid (or a float hair below it) is untouched: 10 kg with a 5 lb Jump is 20 lb, 62.5 kg is 135 lb (not 140), 24 kg is 50 lb, 100 kg is 220 lb, 60 kg is 130 lb, 50 kg with a 10 lb Jump is 110 lb, and lb to kg rounds down the same way (135 lb is 60 kg). Pre-filled rows, untouched live rows after a Settings switch and the Last column round down too, and a set judged after a mid-workout switch is compared with the Target rounded down (3 × 135 lb against 62.5 kg is Clean). A Jump change never snaps a Target: an accepted 62.5 kg stays 62.5 kg after the exercise's Jump changes to 5 kg, and a kg lifter's 12.5 kg baseline stays 12.5 kg with a 2 kg Jump. This covers every kind of Target (Hold, Stay, on track, baseline, accepted). `workout_exercises.target_unit` (migration 0007) records the unit at Start, and `accepted_target_unit` the unit at Go up, so a Go up in lb after a mid-workout switch starts back in kg on the kg grid (140 lb is 62.5 kg, never 63.5 kg); older rows fall back to their sets' `entered_unit`, then kg, and a baseline uses its sets' unit. A step up goes one Jump above the snapped Target, so Go up is always one Jump above Stay (62.5 kg in lb: Stay 135 lb, Go up 140 lb); a drop or deload still rounds down. Bodyweight Targets stay 0, stored sets never change, and a snapped Target restarts the Streak.
- **Step per routine exercise, rounding down, a free rule panel** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-21; rounding down, the per-exercise step and the free rule panel are built): each routine exercise gets its own "Add each time" step, one for kg and one for lb, defaulting to 2.5 kg / 5 lb for every exercise (StrongLifts style). The Weight jumps screen goes; the step is set in the Overload rule panel from the Exercise setup sheet. The whole rule panel becomes free. After a unit switch, a weight from the other unit rounds **down** to the step (62.5 kg is 135 lb); the step is the routine exercise's Jump (see "Jump per routine exercise" above, which replaced "Jump per equipment"). This replaced the "A Target set in the other unit snaps to the Jump's grid" entry (nearest, a tie rounding up), now "A Target set in the other unit rounds down to the Jump's grid" above, and the free/Pro split for rules: the "Overload rules" row is gone from the free/Pro table, since every rule is free. The panel's other values are fixed (owner decision 2026-11-21): a rep step of 1, 3 clean workouts to earn, the miss rule "2 misses in a row: drop 10%", and a Deload every 6 weeks at 60%. See `.scratch/phase-2/exercise-steps/spec.md`.
- **History is per Workout** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-20): a list of finished Workouts, each opening to its exercises and sets. A per-exercise History tab on Exercise detail was built and dropped, because the Target, the live workout's Last column and the Progress tab already cover one exercise over time. See `.scratch/phase-2/map.md`.
- **Progress tab shows records per exercise** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-22): the tab has an exercise picker, a top-set hero with a "New record" pill and a blurred chart preview for free users, and one Records card (Heaviest set, Estimated 1-rep max, Most volume) with a "New" pill on a record set in the last workout. A bodyweight exercise has no weight, so it shows Most reps in a set and Most reps in a workout instead. The weight / est. max / volume toggle and "Last 8" are dropped for free users, since charts are Pro. Why: records were a few stat tiles beside a chart the free tier cannot use. See `docs/screens.md` and `.scratch/phase-2/map.md`.
- **Records rules** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-22): records belong to the exercise across all routines and are never stored. Heaviest set is the highest weight, and more reps at the same weight also counts; Estimated 1-rep max is Epley, a 1-rep set is its own weight and sets over 12 reps are ignored for it only; Most volume is the most weight × reps on one exercise in one workout. A bodyweight exercise has Most reps in a set and Most reps in a workout. Only done Working sets of finished workouts count (warm-ups, unchecked sets and discarded or open workouts do not); a Deload counts normally. The first workout of an exercise sets a silent baseline, and matching a best is not a record. A short "New" pill per record shows on Past workout and the Workout summary, not on History rows. Heaviest set shows as History shows a set; 1-rep max and volume show as whole numbers. See `.scratch/phase-2/records/spec.md`.
- **Progress tab rules** (Phase 2, owner decision 2026-11-22): Progress opens on the most recently done exercise (else the first routine exercise); the picker lists routine exercises plus anything ever done, most recently done first, with "Not done yet" for the rest and a search field above 8; the pick is kept in memory only and cleared when a workout is finished. The hero line is "up / down / same as / up N reps since {date}" or "first workout", with no special case for a Deload. The chart preview and "See all records" opened one "comes with Pro, in a later update" sheet until Phase 3 (replaced by the Paywall, see "Charts and All records"). "New" comes from the last workout's record tags, so a first workout shows none. Why: nothing is stored and the Paywall does not exist yet. See `.scratch/phase-2/progress-tab/spec.md`.
- **Live workout**: saved from Start, one in progress at a time, and its routine is locked until it is finished or discarded; see `.scratch/phase-1/live-workout/spec.md`.
- **Live workout inputs**: weight and reps are typed into plain number inputs. There are no −/+ weight buttons (built, then removed because the owner did not like the interface), no Add set and no remove-set control: the set count is fixed to the plan. A set cannot be marked done while its weight is blank.
- **Offer and summary**: after Finish, each Earned exercise gets its own Offer screen with only Go up and Stay, one at a time, then a summary reports the result with no buttons. A tap saves at once and is final; back and swipe are disabled on Offer screens; unanswered Offers are Stay. A grouped list, "go up on all", a compact card and flipping an answer were sketched and set aside. See `.scratch/phase-1/summary-and-offer/spec.md`.
- **Week start**: the week starts on Sunday in every day row, with three-letter names (Sun, Mon, Tue) (the "Repeat on" picker, the onboarding schedule and the Today week strip), like a calendar. Days are still stored as a bitmask with Monday = bit 0; only the display order changes.
- **Today**: one Routine shows at a time as a swipeable deck (the lifter does one routine per session); a rest day has no Start ("Train anyway", "Start an empty workout", ready-made plans and a week number from the design were left out, the settings gear arrives in Phase 2); a workout belongs to the day it was started, in local time. See `.scratch/phase-1/today/spec.md`.
- **Rest timer**: one timer, started by marking a set done and held as an end time; a buzz at zero and a local notification in the background; -15s and +15s change that rest only; the permission is asked once at the first rest until Phase 4 onboarding asks. See `.scratch/phase-1/rest-timer/spec.md`.
- **Crash resume**: a cold launch reopens the workout in progress (last edit within 12 hours) on its first exercise with an open set; the "edited" row marks are saved so a restart behaves like none. The rest timer is deliberately not saved (decided 2026-11-17: simpler, and a rest after a kill is rarely worth restoring). Offers and the summary are not resumed. See `.scratch/phase-1/crash-resume/spec.md`.
- **Unit switch confirmation**: in Settings, tapping the other unit opens a confirmation sheet saying what changes (weights about to be lifted round down to each exercise's step so every converted weight stays loadable, logged sets stay as typed, an in-progress workout's untouched sets follow); the unit saves only on confirm, and Cancel or swiping down saves nothing.
- **Pro purchase** (Phase 3 feature 1, owner decisions 2026-11-24): the phone's store decides Pro, asked on launch, on return to the app, after a purchase and after Restore purchase; the `entitlements` row is only the fallback when the store can't answer ([ADR 0004](adr/0004-the-store-decides-pro.md)). Bought with expo-iap as one non-consumable, `pro_lifetime`, the same ID on both stores. A refund switches Pro off but everything the lifter made stays; only Pro actions lock. The Locked routine moves to feature 2 (it is not built here). Pro unlocked never shows over a live workout: it is owed (`unlock_screen_owed`) until the workout is finished or discarded, or a later launch that resumes none. A dev-only "Pro source" switch (Store / Pro / Free, in memory, resets each launch, never writes the row) lets a dev build try both states. Why: a hand-edited cache must not unlock Pro for good, and a new phone must get Pro back without paying again. See `.scratch/phase-3/pro-purchase/spec.md`.
- **Routine limit upgrade** (Phase 3 feature 2, owner decisions 2026-11-24): without Pro, routines past the 2 oldest by creation (lowest id; reordering never changes it) are Locked routines, worked out on read and never stored. The Routines tab swaps its meter for "N routines locked. Unlock Pro to use them again." (opens the Paywall) and dims locked rows with a lock and a grey Pro tag. A locked routine opens read-only (banner, Unlock Pro as the bottom button, ⋯ offers Delete only) and can't be started. Today and Reminders leave locked routines out, and a day with only locked routines is a plain rest day with no nudge. A workout started before the lock finishes completely, including Offers. The limit pop-up pitches Pro (See Pro / Not now, no mention of deleting) and, after buying, the lifter lands back on the Routines tab. Why: a refund must never cost the lifter anything they made. See `.scratch/phase-3/routine-limit-upgrade/spec.md`.
- **Custom exercises** (Phase 3 feature 3, owner decisions 2026-11-25): Pro creates and edits them; a free lifter, including after a refund, still uses existing ones everywhere and can delete them, and can open and fill in New exercise and Edit, but the save button is replaced by **Unlock Pro**, which opens the Paywall (owner decision 2026-11-25). A name can't match a library or custom exercise (trimmed, ignoring case, 1 to 40 characters). Fields: name, one main muscle group (stored as the lowercase group name), equipment (Barbell, Dumbbell, Machine, Cable, Bodyweight, Other) and optional how-to steps (up to 10 of 200 characters). Equipment decides the load type: Bodyweight is reps only, anything else is weight × reps; there is no Time tracking. Once an exercise is in a routine or has a logged set, Equipment can't be switched to or from Bodyweight. Delete is a hard delete that cascades and removes the exercise's history for good ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-deleting-a-custom-exercise-deletes-its-history.md)); it is blocked while the exercise is in a Workout in progress. Entry points: **+** in the Library header and the no-match "Create" row (Library and routine picker), open for everyone. Why: the lifter owns what they made and chose to remove, and one rule (equipment) keeps sets, records and rules consistent. See `.scratch/phase-3/custom-exercises/spec.md`.
- **Charts and All records** (Phase 3 feature 4, owner decisions 2026-11-25): a Pro lifter's Progress hero card shows a live chart in place of the blurred preview (variant A, line + area, picked from three prototypes), and "See all records" opens an All records screen. The chart has a metric toggle above (Weight / Est. 1RM / Volume; a bodyweight exercise gets Reps in a set / Reps in a workout), opens on Weight (or Reps in a set) and Last 8, and a range dropdown under it (tap the pill, pick from a sheet with a tick) chooses Last 8 / Last 20 / All. One point per finished workout with a value for the metric, oldest first, using the Records rules (done working sets only, kg compared in kg, the 12-rep cap for the 1-rep max); a workout with no value is skipped, never 0, and "Last N" means the latest N plottable workouts. Tapping a dot shows its date and value. With fewer than 2 plottable points a muted line replaces the chart ("Your chart starts after your second workout.", or "No Est. 1RM yet: every set was over 12 reps."). All records lists only exercises with a counted set, most recently done first, with muscle group chips, a "14 records · 2 new" banner ("this week" dropped) and read-only cards; "New" is the Progress rule. A free lifter, including after a refund, keeps the blurred preview and the Pro-tagged row, and tapping either opens the Paywall (the "comes with Pro, in a later update" sheet of Progress tab rules is gone); after buying they land back on Progress with nothing opening by itself. Drawn with react-native-svg, no new dependency; reads are free and only the chart and the screen are gated, in the one Pro-gate place. Nothing is stored, no schema change, no ADR (reversible). Out of scope: pinch zoom, a date-range picker, comparing exercises, tapping a card to open the exercise. Why: charts and an All records screen are what Pro lifters pay for, and a dead-end sheet in front of the Paywall lost the sale. See `.scratch/phase-3/charts-and-all-records/spec.md`.

- **Backup and restore** (Phase 3 feature 6, owner decisions 2026-11-26): Create backup is Pro; Restore from a backup is free for everyone and sits on Your data, because a new phone starts Free and restores the purchase after the file. The `.increment` file is plain JSON (no password), holds finished workouts, routines, custom exercises and settings, and leaves out the Pro entitlement (the store decides, ADR 0004) and any Workout in progress. Restore is blocked while a Workout in progress exists, checks the whole file first (a newer format version or an invalid file is refused), then replaces everything in one all-or-nothing step, and re-plans reminders without asking for permission. "Last backup" updates only when the share sheet reports the share finished. On Android (owner decision 2026-11-26) the share sheet has no "Save to device" and can't report its outcome, so Backup ready offers a primary **Save to a folder** (system folder picker, then the file is written there) and a secondary **Share**; there "Last backup" is set only when the folder save succeeds, and sharing never sets it. A cancelled folder pick changes nothing and shows no error; a failed write shows a plain error. iOS keeps the single Save or share file button. Your data shows only working rows; features 7 and 8 add theirs. Why: the gate is "a backup restores on a new phone", and a restore must never half-apply or hand out Pro. See `.scratch/phase-3/backup-and-restore/spec.md`.
- **Automatic backups** (Phase 3 feature 7, owner decisions 2026-11-26): a Pro lifter's app writes a `.increment` file by itself and keeps the last 3 / 5 / 10 / 20 (default 5). iOS saves to the app's own folder (visible in Files, no iCloud Drive, no new dependency); Android to a folder the lifter picks (the picker opens when the switch is first turned on). After every workout writes when the workout is finished, with an open-time catch-up; daily, weekly and monthly are due on a rolling 1 / 7 / 30 days since the last backup and only if the data changed, decided by a content fingerprint (`last_backup_hash` in `settings`, no migration). Manual and automatic backups share the one Last backup date. Automatic files are named `Increment auto backup {date} {HH-mm}.increment` and pruning deletes only those. Back up now always writes. A failed write shows one alert per failure streak plus a status line and never blocks Finish or launch. A free lifter sees a locked row that opens the Paywall; after a refund it pauses and settings and files stay. "Changed" for After every workout's open-time catch-up is a cheap check (a workout finished since the last backup); the other frequencies build the export once and compare its fingerprint, and a manual backup stores the fingerprint too. When a workout summary closes, a backup that came due while it was open is not lost. Why: a backup nobody remembers to make is missing on the day the phone is lost. See `.scratch/phase-3/automatic-backups/spec.md`.
- **The iOS database lives outside Documents** (Phase 3 feature 7, owner decision 14, 2026-11-26): `app.json` shows `Documents` in Files (for the backup files), and expo-sqlite's default iOS folder is `Documents/SQLite`, so the live database would be visible and deletable there. The database opens in `Library/Application Support` on iOS, with a one-time safe move of an existing `increment.db`, `-wal` and `-shm` (never overwrites, old files deleted only after the copies are size-checked, the old location stays in use if the move fails). Android is unchanged. Backups stay in `Documents`. Why: a lifter tidying Files must not be able to delete their workouts.
- **Export and delete all data** (Phase 3 feature 8, owner decisions 2026-11-27): Export your progress is Pro and is one row on Your data that opens Export progress with Spreadsheet ticked; a Format picker switches to PDF report, and a Time range of 30 days, 3 months or All time (default All time, by workout date) applies to both. A free lifter sees the row locked with a Pro tag; it opens the Paywall and lands back on Your data after buying; after a refund it locks again and anything already exported stays. The CSV holds every done set of finished workouts (warm-ups included and marked in `is_warmup`; skipped sets and a Workout in progress left out) with date, workout, exercise, set, reps, `weight` as typed, `unit` and `weight_kg` to two decimals, so nothing is rounded to a Jump or converted (ADR 0003). Builder choices: a name starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets a leading `'` so a spreadsheet never runs it as a formula, and the file starts with a UTF-8 BOM so Excel reads non-ASCII names. The PDF holds a totals header, one Records table and a Weight over time chart per exercise (Reps in a set for bodyweight; the Progress chart's point rules, a muted line under 2 points), no workout log. It is HTML rendered by `expo-print` (the one new dependency, added with `npx expo install`), names are HTML-escaped and a block never splits across pages. Files are `Increment export {date}.csv` and `Increment report {date}.pdf`, handed to the share sheet. Delete all data is free for everyone, a red link on Your data. It is blocked while a Workout in progress exists ("Finish or discard your workout first.", checked when it opens, before any typing); otherwise a sheet shows the counts and "Your Pro purchase isn't affected.", and the lifter types DELETE (ignoring case and spaces) to enable Delete everything. It removes workouts, sets, routines and Custom exercises in one all-or-nothing step, resets preferences to defaults and cancels reminders, switches automatic backups off and clears Last backup and the fingerprint. It keeps the Pro entitlement (Pro is never reset), the library exercises and every backup file already saved, and never deletes files on disk. A "Create a backup first" shortcut shows for Pro lifters only, so the sheet never opens the Paywall; Restore from a backup stays free. The lifter stays on Your data (0 workouts, 0 sets, 0 records, "No backup yet", "All your data was deleted."); whether it re-opens onboarding is a Phase 4 open item. No ADR: reversible, nothing stored, no schema change. Why: a lifter's history should be readable outside the app, and clearing it should never cost them what they paid for. See `.scratch/phase-3/export-and-delete/spec.md`.
